How close it gets

Each track's light was computed offline by the real pipeline from a silent capture of the exact upload the player streams, so the light and the music stay in step. Only the colours, the section calls and the marks are stored here, never audio. Music mode is far from perfect: on 402 Creative Commons tracks it had not trained on, it finds about one breakdown start in ten. Every number, and what is still wrong, is in the research notes.
